CGC Midautumn Dice Game

Dice combinations

Roll 6 dice. Red pips are 4s, a dashed die means any number. A roll that fits two prizes wins the higher one.

Game rules

Rolling

  • The group keeps rolling until the prizes run out.
  • After the prizes run out, there is one last round of rolls, ending with the person who won the last prize.

1st prize special rule

  • The 1st prize winner loses their prize if someone else rolls a higher 1st prize combination.
  • They also lose it if they roll a new 1st prize combination that is lower than another person’s.

Prizes per table

Every table gets the same set of prizes.

87 prizes per table

Held as reserve, not given to tables: 4 Jin Ramyun, 2 Chippy.
